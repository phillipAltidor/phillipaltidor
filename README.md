## Professional Overview

My interest in technology started at a young age when my dad gave me an old work laptop with outdated enterprise software. After running into problems with the system, I spent hours troubleshooting it and eventually figured out how to reinstall Windows from a USB on my own. That experience sparked the curiosity I still have today for understanding how technology works behind the scenes.

As a developer, I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into something functional and useful, especially when I can make features work the way I envisioned them. I’m particularly interested in full stack development and understanding how each part of an application connects, from the frontend to the backend and database. I’m eager to continue learning, take on new challenges, and find an opportunity where I can contribute while developing my skills alongside experienced developers.
